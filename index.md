## Detect Real Hunger

It is normal to eat food when you are hungry, but do you know when you are actually hungry? Can you know the difference between hunger and craving?
Here are the three important points to remember before eating:

1. Stomach: Does my Stomach actually feel empty?
1. Energy: Do I feel like I need food, or am I feeling normal?
1. Any food: Would I eat a basic meal right now?

Even simpler: remember S. E. F. - Stomach, Energy, Food.
If 2-3 are yes, you're probably physically hungry.

Remembering these points can help save time and money and prevent unnecessary purchases and waste of food. These points apply to any age and any situation in our lives. 
Also remember to drink water to stay hydrated and fill up. Also have plenty of sleep.
